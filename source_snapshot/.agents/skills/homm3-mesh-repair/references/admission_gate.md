# Candidate Admission Gate

Run this short check before expensive fitting and again against the exact retained bind. It prevents a technically renderable candidate from being mistaken for a source-compatible repair.

1. **Canonical identity:** open the exact variant's local VCMI/HoMM3 portrait PNG and its source manifest before web research; an article or search result is not the portrait. Match the base/upgrade/alternate game SID to its portrait entry. Use the web only for attributable high-resolution original renders or documented secondary art. Compare prepared input and generated mesh against the viewed portrait, full-body sprite silhouette, material/color placement, face, appendages and equipment, plus the best existing candidate in matched views. An easier bind does not excuse a less authentic body; record the tradeoff and retain the best available mesh.
2. **Motion and rig:** pin source sequence, capture/skeleton, body plan, joint correspondence, and authored rest. For every non-body moving part, name the source event/motion owner and the candidate geometry owner. Joint count or a correctly named but static/unweighted bone is not compatibility. A winged body needs compatible body motion as well as wing motion.
3. **Facing and pivots:** compare canonical source plates and candidate rest in front and side views. Record forward axis, facing, scale, ground/pivot, and anatomical pivots. Do not repair a unit-specific mismatch by a shared left/right or camera flip.
4. **Equipment:** pin exact source/donor mesh, source component/face selection, owner, grip/attachment transform, held/released policy, and visible/occluded axis assumptions. Inspect both isolated prop and remaining body from multiple sides. A prop that follows a hand can still be fragmented or contaminated; one connected component is not proof of purity.
5. **Selected bind:** after the final bind/weights are chosen, check the actual geometry assigned to every required moving owner and any released part. Run the selected candidate through its own saved motion. Never inherit an ownership pass from another bind, mesh, or body plan.
6. **Playback readiness:** before the full run, verify a local preview route can open the intended media, or identify a fallback viewer/conversion. If playback remains unavailable, still retain the complete best-effort equipped reel and exact-candidate metrics, reject acceptance, and record full continuity review as unverified. Missing viewer access is not a reason to stop at an audit or omit the reel.

The Goblin attempt-1 positive demonstrates the value of exact unit rest/motion plus source-mapped equipment, a pinned hand/grip, explicit held/released and occlusion assumptions, four-view isolation, and an equipped reel. The Gargoyle attempt-2 same-family body-delta transfer retained its own mesh, bind pose and wing track but did not resolve visible source-motion deformation; family/joint/event similarity alone is not a compatibility pass. The Mage attempt-2 color-filtered staff cut reduced the selected corridor from 356 to 149 faces yet retained gaps and robe fragments, and its attempt-3 equipped replay remained rejected. These are bounded rejection findings, not universal repair recipes. Later Gargoyle attempt 3 and Stone Golem's final tier were selected as best-effort candidates with disclosed defects; selection is not pristine or runtime proof. Do not transplant Goblin assets or unit-specific masks to another unit.
