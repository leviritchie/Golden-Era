## HotA Unit Stats And Abilities

All Necropolis creatures are Undead. HotA Undead expectations:

- unaffected by Morale
- immune to mind spells, with the HotA Forgetfulness fix included
- immune to Bless, Curse, Death Ripple, Resurrection, and Sacrifice
- valid targets for Animate Dead and Destroy Undead
- not valid targets for Vampire Lord life drain
- not damaged by Lich death cloud

## Laws And Faction Skill

Faction skill proposal: OE native `Necromancy` (`skill_faction_undead`), adjusted so HoMM3 Necropolis units are valid recipients.

Required release behavior:

- Basic/Advanced/Expert HotA Necromancy raises 5%/10%/15% of killed enemy creatures as Skeletons or Skeleton Warriors, subject to HoMM3 slot, HP-efficiency, and upgrade rules.
- Necromancy specialists scale the secondary-skill effect by the HotA specialist formula if supported by OE skill math.
- Native OE post-battle undead HP conversion is acceptable only if the design owner explicitly chooses an OE-style Necropolis. Until then, exact HotA Skeleton raising remains the target.

Potential law themes, to be specified after mechanics are traced:

- Grave levy: Skeleton growth or Necromancy conversion modifier.
- Rotting ranks: Zombie durability or Disease reliability.
- Wraith chill: Wight/Wraith regeneration or mana pressure.
- Blood court: Vampire Lord life drain cap or sustain.
- Lich rites: Death cloud targeting or ranged damage.
- Dragon ossuary: Bone/Ghost Dragon cost, morale, or aging support.

## Heroes

- Clone Undead hero/subclass scaffolding only as a structural donor.
- Class labels: Death Knight and Necromancer.
- Unit specialists must apply to both base and upgraded creatures following HotA specialty rules.
- Necromancy specialists must affect the HotA Necromancy percent and must be tested against `h3_*` undead eligibility.
- Spell specialists must use existing OE dark/death spell SIDs only when the spell equivalent is mechanically close enough; otherwise record a spell-design debt.

## Town Art

- Necropolis town has many dark overlays and animated effects. Generate layered town art and mark fog, glow, and Dragon Vault animation layers for visual review.
- Skeleton Transformer and Cover of Darkness visuals must not imply implemented behavior if the behavior is still a static-trace debt. The UI should either expose the actual mechanic or keep those buildings descriptive only.

- The source plugin already has Necropolis unit import rows, custom unit bundles, static unit portraits, preview manifests, lab sidecars, faction icon/card art, and city/dwelling map-object reference frames. Do not keep stale "no bundles/import payload" release blocks unless validation regresses.
- Necropolis release hardening still needs `homm3_hero_portraits/`, town layers, build-tree icons, release UI registry/selector exposure, and mechanics validation. Core.zip data alone is still not enough for visible faction UI or release readiness.

## Unique Faction Mechanics

- `Necromancy`: HotA target is Skeleton/Skeleton Warrior raising from killed enemy creatures, not generic post-battle HP conversion, unless explicitly redesigned.
- `Skeleton Transformer`: required town mechanic debt for creature conversion.
- `Cover of Darkness`: required map-visibility debt.
- `Undead`: all seven lines must share the HotA undead immunity and eligibility profile.
- Disease, Curse, Death Blow, and Aging are part of the HotA unit definitions, but their source chance-on-hit behavior must be adapted as guaranteed focus-point-costing active abilities in Olden Era. Preserve their legal targets, effect strength, timing constraints, duration, and removal/immunity rules; do not ship them as hidden RNG passives and do not omit them for convenience.

## Static Trace Debts

- HoMM3 `h3_*` unit eligibility in OE native Necromancy and Undead systems.
- Exact HotA Skeleton/Skeleton Warrior raising, including empty-slot and 2/3 Skeleton Warrior conversion rules.
- Necromancy Amplifier and Soul Prison global allied-hero Necromancy bonuses.
- Skeleton Transformer conversion UI/session action.
- Cover of Darkness map shroud/vision behavior.
- Undead morale and spell-immunity rules if not donor-backed.
- Zombie Disease: focus-active target eligibility, duration, Cure/Dispel removal, level-2 immunity interactions, and preservation of the source -2 Attack/-2 Defense strength.
- Wight/Wraith Regeneration timing and 50 HP cap.
- Wraith per-stack mana drain timing and hero mana target.
- Vampire/Vampire Lord no-retaliation and Vampire Lord life drain cap/eligibility.
- Lich/Power Lich death cloud adjacency, living-only filter, ally damage, two-hex handling, and ranged-only condition.
- Black Knight/Dread Knight/Dreadtaur Curse focus-active target eligibility, Advanced Curse/source strength, timing, and magical-terrain interactions.
- Dread Knight and Guard-line Death Blow/Death Strike focus-active base-damage-only doubling.
- Bone/Ghost Dragon non-cumulative enemy morale reduction.
- Ghost Dragon Aging focus-active max-HP halving, rounding, duration, living-only eligibility, and Cure/Dispel removal.
- Spectral Dragon and Dracolich Death Stare focus-active target legality and source-strength-10 kill/count math.
