# Surface: HoMM3 skinned-mesh battle unit

## Intent

Ship a HoMM3 custom unit’s **battle**, **unit preview**, and **adventure-map** presentation as a Unity skinned mesh with bone clips, not as a billboard `Texture2DArray`. Preview and map both loop Idle. Donor `UnitViewConfig.scale` stays the intended size.

This is a **presentation lane**, not a Core ability/law overlay. Do not invent a second battle-visual owner.

## Ownership preference

1. **Fit clips** from the delivery mesh. Idle-0 mocap binds a Mixamo / Eagle / hybrid / zoo / mounted donor into that mesh: a canonical SmartRig when one exists, otherwise a meshy7 generated mesh. A canonical SmartRig is not required for mocap. SmartRig GPU-cascade pose search (`smartrig_cascade`) is allowed only when that donor is totally unviable and a canonical SmartRig exists. Crusader's named-clip exception still documents `run_gpu_cascade_animation_set.py` as historical delivery. Oriented GLBs use `Orientation` −90° yaw in glTF so Unity imports **+90°**.
2. **Unity lab** `Homm3OwnedSkinnedLabBuild` (canonical: `tools/homm3_import/unity_editor/Homm3OwnedSkinnedLabBuild.cs`) builds the owned prefab, rebases clip paths, rescales to stock Esquire durations, sets `applyRootMotion = false`, stamps `__homm3_skinned_battle_lane__`. Crusader keeps `CrusaderOwnedSkinnedLabBuild` as the named-clip exception.
3. **Bundle** `BuildCustomUnitBundles.BuildFromManifest` with `battlePresentationLane: "skinned_mesh"` (canonical builder: `tools/custom_prefab_pipeline/unity_editor/BuildCustomUnitBundles.cs`). Map prefab is the same OwnedSkinned prefab (looping Idle), not a billboard.
4. **Core overlay** `normalize_custom_unit_view_skinned_preview_meshes` points `UnitViewConfig.mesh` / `mapMesh` at `homm3_import/...` like vanilla `Humans/1Prefabs/esquire`. Requires `homm3ImportPreviewHooks=true` so synthetic preview loads.
5. **Plugin** `Homm3ImportVisuals` + release/pool/facing hooks recognize the marker, skip billboard map finish for skinned instances, copy donor map renderer flags onto the owned mesh, hide the donor shell drivers, and play Idle on the adventure-map visual. The shell keeps no Animator outside the owned visual, so the native map-squad drawer binds the owned one and plays its `Unit_Death` when the stack is killed (`docs/homm3_map_squad_death_contract.md`, gate `tools/validate_homm3_map_squad_death_contract.py`).

Playbook with session evidence: `docs/crusader_owned_bind_skinned_mesh_handoff.md`. Pose fit: `.cursor/skills/homm3-pose-pipeline/SKILL.md`.

**Crusader this iteration (2026-08-17):** Meshy smartrig mesh `artifacts/meshy_downloads/h3_crusader_smartrigged_rigidized.glb` (user override; owned-bind hard gate waived for this ship). Delivery tree: `artifacts/crusader_pose_pipeline/gpu_cascade_skinned/`. DEF groups **7 and 8** (turn left/right), **11/13** (attack up/down), and **14/16** (shoot up/down) are skipped — Olden Era 3D space has no vertical attack/shoot variants. **2** ships as `Crusader_Idle` → `22.Idle` / `23.Idle_Rare`. **1** (mouseover) fills stock `15–19.Skill_*`. **12** is the only melee attack clip. **15** ShootFront ships only when the unit DEF has it. Move-start/end map to fly/stance transitions.

## Pitfalls

- **Idle-0 mocap is preferable when the donor is viable. A canonical SmartRig is not required for that bind.** Use Mixamo / Eagle / hybrid / zoo / mounted idle-0 bind-first whenever that bind can contain the delivery mesh, including meshy7 generated meshes that have no SmartRig. `wait_smartrig` is only for `smartrig_cascade` without a SmartRig. Missing or quarantined meshes use `wait_mesh`. Do not leave Water Elemental or Cove Swashbuckler on the billboard path because they lack a SmartRig. If the donor cannot contain the mesh (joint outside the volume, or the same class of hard anatomy failure) and a canonical SmartRig exists, pose those SmartRig bones with GPU cascade (`smartrig_cascade` → `run_gpu_cascade_animation_set.py`) and stage `Unit_*.glb` under `remaining_forced_smartrig_cascade`. Do not cascade leftover or fail-closed units that still have a viable passthrough donor. Do not reopen silhouette pose-search as the catalog lane. Current cascade case: `h3_hydra` (`artifacts/meshy_downloads/h3_hydra_smartrigged.glb`) after zoo-dragon remapping left a joint outside the mesh (`skeleton_outside_mesh_front_side_top`). Chaos Hydra and Ruinous Hydra keep their completed zoo-dragon clips. Proof: generated-artifact. Not OE battle.
- **Idle-0 bind is a floor column.** After Mixamo facing is undone onto camera +Z (snapped 45°), every ground-contact joint (1, 2, 4, or more feet) is pinned to the mesh floor and the skeleton is height-scaled so the top joint meets the mesh top. The foot centroid is placed on the floor footprint, then yaw-rotated until that footprint fits (prefer a yaw that also fits the top). Joints are then walked lowest-to-highest and moved only in X and Z into the local center of the nearest filled mesh slice; Y does not change. Empty height bands fail closed. Mocap still owns later-frame relative swings. Frame 0 of playback is this bind pose. Proof: generated-artifact registration + bind report, not OE runtime. A named `linked_master_ground` experiment does only the floor drop and a uniform height scale (no per-bone move, no yaw search). A later `linked_master_scale` experiment starts from the previous yaw-fix bind and applies the bind-lab scale slider (uniform scale about the hips, mesh stays) until the lowest feet reach the mesh floor. Those experiments violate bind-first containment on purpose and are not the live idle-0 default. Units with no frozen donor compare the demo Mixamo person and Eagle flyer after that same yaw-fix + hip-scale recipe and keep the lower mean vertex-to-bone distance; a tie or both failing is closed. Remaining units are then classified by Mixamo leftover: a mid-size leftover that Eagle covers while Mixamo still owns the body is the Mixamo-body + Eagle-wing graft (`classify_demo_donor_mix.py`); a one-sided leftover (weapon, pack) keeps Mixamo when Mixamo owns the body even if Eagle's overall mean is slightly lower; a large central leftover is refused as a missing mount donor rather than shipped as Mixamo-only or Eagle-only. The measured remaining-unit inventory is `artifacts/homm3_mocap_v2/full_set_mocap_idle0_v1/donor_mix_inventory_measured.json`; bind-first clips ship through `assemble_remaining_idle0_delivery.py` + `ship_remaining_idle0.py` with `motionAuthority=idle0_bind_first`. A remaining-scale folder with only some `Unit_*.glb` files is not complete — the official master-sequence reel must be present, including `Unit_Move`. Delivery trees hardlink those clip GLBs; do not copy the 28 MB mesh again, do not export unused yaw-fix clip GLBs, and do not snapshot full AssetBundles into `before_build_*` / `installed_backup_*`. After remaining-scale clips exist, keep only the yaw-fix registration JSON. Skip remaining-lane GIFs and `cleanup_edits` dumps. Keep `Source/master.glb`: the owned prefab's skinned mesh and avatar are that import. Deleting it after save ships non-finite rest vertices. Batches stay small enough that ALLOC_GFX does not exhaust the machine. That is not the Stronghold `current_mocap_v2` selection and must not restore the 8-slot sidecar skin path. Offline bundle proof for this lane is `ProbeRemainingIdle0Bundles` (battle-lane marker + source-pinned clip motion). `ProbeMocapDeliveryBundles` still owns the unlimited-skin / 8-influence current-mocap gate. Bind-first shrink aims at the vertex mean, not the AABB center — a crouched mesh can leave the box center outside the flesh. An angel **body+wings hybrid** (`run_angel_hybrid_body_wings.py`) is a separate experiment: keep every Mixamo joint, capture the same reel with Eagle, and copy only Eagle `BN_Wing_*` positions after one bind-frame torso similarity onto `mixamorig:Spine2`. Bind-first then contains and centers only the Mixamo body joints (`containmentJointNames`); grafted `BN_Wing_*` joints do not vote for yaw, pitch, or roll. A vertical spine match does not pin ground-plane yaw. That is not the seated Wolf Rider composer and is not the automatic donor pick. Official `linked_master` stays untouched.
