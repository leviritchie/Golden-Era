## Audit Rules

- Do not clear a row because `xHomm3ProductionDebt.mechanicsDebt` is empty; compare source behavior against the actual `damageDealer`, `targetMechanics`, buff sid/duration, trigger, cleanup, and target filter.
- Do not use stock `add_attack_after_counter*` tags for Ayssid Ferocity. Ferocity is kill-triggered and attack-only, not a generic second strike.
- Do not implement Dendroid Binding by increasing `ent_root` duration alone. Without source-target cleanup, that over-roots targets after the Dendroid moves or dies.
- Do not use Peasant `Taxpayer` view text as proof of a reusable unit economy mechanism. Swashbuckler gold required a live side/session resource trace.
- Do not describe Cove after-shot as “hook not built”; the release-safe slice exists. Remaining debt is combat live-positive proof only.
