# Golden Era v0.1.328 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.327

### Factory: Logistics Network replaces Split Assembly

- A Factory hero with the skill can visit their own mines and dwellings to unlock **enhancements**: extra output, Export Office gold, Warehouses, Mercenary HQ, Support Hub and more. Each building has one active enhancement, which can be switched once a week.
- Buildings link to each other and to towns along real map paths, limited by a **range** measured in days of travel. Logistics Hubs and Rail Stations extend that range. Enemy stacks and fog can cut a link.
- The free **Logistics Network** spell, or **Ctrl+L** on the adventure map, opens a map of the whole network. From there you can build remotely, switch enhancements, see network totals and range, and get an explanation and a fix for every cut route.
- **Recruit all** (B in the viewer) hires from every linked dwelling at once, with Max all and a total cost.
- New subskills: Mobile Command Center (remote builds within the hero's range), Freight Haulers, Survey Office, Night Shift, Supply Caravan (the hero acts as a Logistics Hub) and Trade Contracts (the hero counts as two extra markets).

### Conflux: Elemental Fusion replaces Elemental Conjury

- A world-map spell opens a fusion panel: drag two elemental stacks in to fuse them into one of 105 fused elementals, each with its own look and abilities. Fusion is permanent. The spell is free and has no daily limit, and higher skill ranks unlock more combinations.

### Dungeon: War Captives

- After a won battle, a War Captives window lets you deal with the defeated enemy: ransom them for gold, recruit them, or use the other options your skill unlocks. Recruiting uses a panel styled after the native join window. This replaces the old Captives.

### Fortress: Predator Instinct replaces Hunting Grounds

- At the start of each battle your stacks are paired 1:1 with prey in the enemy army and deal extra damage to it. Hovering a stack draws a line to its prey.
- Subskills: Swamp Tactics, Intimidation (slows initiative), Endurance Hunting, Frenzy, Cautious and Pounce.

### Inferno: Gating redesign

- Gating now holds back stacks from your own army. Reserved stacks skip deployment and step out of a fiery portal in round 2. Gating is always on, and the reserved slots are marked on the hero's army strip.
- New subskills add slots, Haste on arrival, a protective barrier on arrival (Shielded Arrival), and Open Gate, which brings waiting stacks in immediately.

### Other fixes

- Factions on custom terrains (for example Factory's Foundry) now get rare mines, rare resources and guarded banks in randomly generated maps.
- The gold income tooltip includes the mod's extra daily gold.
- Level-up and hero-profile skill icons no longer show a stale custom icon.
- Leftover green backgrounds were removed from painted unit portraits.
- Untranslated mod text now stays fully in English instead of mixing in partly translated words.
- Troll Defense raised from 10 to 11 to fit its tier.

## Known issues

- Many of the new faction skills above have only had a short in-game test. Saving and reloading with an active Logistics Network has not been checked yet. Please report anything that behaves differently from its description.
- On the Blitz template, rare mines sit in the guarded side zones, not in your start zone. This is the template's original design.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.
