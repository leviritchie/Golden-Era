# Golden Era v0.1.321 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has a lot of issues. Castle animations got a full pass in this release, and the Rampart pass has started, but many units in the other factions still have unfinished animations.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.320

### Castle animations (HoMM3-authentic motion pass)

Every Castle unit was reviewed against the original HoMM3 animations, from both the battle and preview views.

- **Pikeman / Halberdier:** level pike thrust with a lunge, and the pike stays upright in the hit reaction. Halberdier fingers stay on the hand, with no stray chip near the grip.
- **Archer / Marksman:** the crossbow is raised level at the enemy when shooting. The stock no longer sticks to the thigh. The hover lift and the defend guard are back. Loose hip and leg fragments are removed.
- **Crossbowman:** aims from shoulder height like HoMM3. The crossbow no longer flips during hit reactions or melee attacks. The quiver stays on the hip.
- **Swordsman:** the walk swings the sword from the arm, not the wrist. Fist and hilt fragments no longer float off the belt.
- **Guardsman:** the helm stays upright while walking (no more sideways head tilt).
- **Monk / Zealot / Priest:** the arms are freed from the sleeves, so casts reach out, and the head stays on the target.
- **Cavalier / Champion, Angel / Archangel, Crusader, Justiciar, Seraph, Glaivier:** attack lunges, hit reactions and gaze toward the target reworked per unit.
- **Griffin / Royal Griffin / Battle Griffin:** folded-wing idle restored and flight reworked. Royal and Battle Griffins are recolours of the Griffin.

### Rampart animations (pass in progress)

- **Centaur, Unicorn, Wood Elf, Grand Elf:** reworked against HoMM3. The elves' bow arms are freed from the body, so the bow draws at the target instead of sitting in front of the face.
- **Green Dragon / Gold Dragon:** larger wing spans with articulated wings. Defence and Death no longer push the wing tips through the floor or past the tail.
- **Dwarf / Battle Dwarf:** overhead windup into a ground smash with a deep crouch, like HoMM3. The hammer no longer drags strips of the belly or hip along with it, and the handle no longer splinters.
- **Dendroid Guard:** wide backhand branch sweep at the enemy, arms flung wide when hit, and a shielding arm in Defence. The arms no longer stretch to the thighs when raised.
- The remaining Rampart units are unchanged in this release.

### Fixes

- Fixed the mod failing to load on systems that use a decimal comma (for example many European locales). This also covers black town screens on those systems.
- Fixed battle hero portraits sometimes showing another hero's face.
- Fixed the vanilla Grove town showing an old placeholder backdrop.
- Fixed a full-3D town (Castle) going black when one building couldn't set up its click targets.
- Restored custom faction effects that stopped working on Steam build 25672315:
  - Castle Elite Strategem (Spread Out / Compress / Ambush)
  - Dungeon Draconic Dominion
  - Tower Mana Well Aegis
  - Necropolis Skeletal Necromancy sub-skill upgrades
  - Marshal Suppressing Fire trap damage
  - Conflux spell-copy visual
  - Cove Disrupting Ray
  - Bulwark Runes gains, Split Assembly, Jotunn Slow and the Focus grants
- Map-defined prison heroes, custom dwelling hires and billboards, and the campaign briefings that played twice after a failed load and retry also work again.
- Main menu: a bug report banner (press F1) that hides after 10 seconds.

## Known issues

- The Castle and Rampart animation changes have not been checked in game yet.
- Royal and Battle Griffin wing fan differs from HoMM3.
- Dendroid Guard Death: the body disappears when it bursts, instead of collapsing into a heap of logs.
- Other factions' animations are still unfinished.
