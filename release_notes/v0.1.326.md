# Golden Era v0.1.326 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues. Most units have had an animation pass against the original HoMM3 animations, but some are still being polished, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.325

### Movement and flight animations for about 120 units

This release is mostly a movement pass. Walking units got new Move animations with a steadier body, weapons held instead of swinging loose, and tails, trunks and cloaks that follow the motion. Flying units got new flight cycles with wings that actually beat. Where the old mesh could not make the motion without tearing, the mesh and its rig were repaired first.

- **Castle:** Griffin and Battle Griffin (new wing joints and flight), Angel, Archangel, Seraph, Cavalier, Justiciar.
- **Rampart:** Centaur line, Unicorn, War Unicorn, Pegasus line, Diamond Dragon.
- **Tower:** Gremlin line, Genie and Master Genie, Giant, Colossus, Battle Mage, Obsidian and Elemental Gargoyle.
- **Inferno:** Gog line, Demon line, Pit Fiend line, Efreet and Efreet Sultan, Vermin.
- **Necropolis:** Skeleton Warrior and Archer, Zombie, Lich line, Black Knight line, Banshee, Bone Dragon, Spectral Dragon.
- **Dungeon:** Troglodyte line, Harpy and Harpy Hag, Evil Eye line, Manticore, Scorpicore, Chimera.
- **Stronghold:** Wolf Rider and Raider, Ogre Mage, Cyclops, Roc, Thunderbird, Shahrokh.
- **Fortress:** Gnoll, Lizardman variants, Dragon Fly line, Basilisk line, Wyvern, Hydra line.
- **Conflux:** Water, Frost, Storm, Hurricane, Psychic and Astral Elementals, Phoenix.
- **Cove:** Nix line, Nereid, Sea Serpent line, Stormbird, Ayssid, Halcyon.
- **Factory:** Armadillo line, Bounty Hunter, Sandworm variant, Couatl line.
- **Bulwark:** Kobold Foreman, Mountain Ram, Snow Elf line, Yeti line, Shaman line, Mammoth line, Jotunn variants.
- **Neutral:** Boar, Nomad, Troll, Fangarm, Faerie Dragon, Fury.

### Faction and unit abilities

A review of the custom faction and unit abilities fixed a number of wrong assumptions in the code:

- **Cove Plunder**, **Fortress Hunting Grounds**, Necropolis raising after battle and several faction laws could fire twice or be skipped after loading a save. They are now counted once per chest or battle.
- **Castle Elite Stratagem** now only triggers, and only spends its token, against armies without a hero.
- **Necromancy** no longer raises from Necropolis units, elementals, golems or gargoyles.
- **Bulwark** rune effects are tied to the action that caused them, and the flyer slow now applies to the correct side.
- **Dungeon Captives** and **Draconic Dominion** find the right hero and keep their tier in step.
- Attacks marked "no retaliation" no longer trigger counterattacks, a duplicate Gremlin repair ability was removed, and a few ability texts were corrected (Harpy, Pikeman, Walking Dead).

### Other fixes

- Fixed a case where modded terrain could turn the native ground blank, and one where re-applied terrain tables counted water tiles twice.
- Build-tree icons no longer force hidden card overlays to show.
- Metal shine now reads its settings correctly on systems that use a comma as the decimal separator.
- Bug reports now include the last 2 MB of the log instead of 256 KB.

## Known issues

- The ability fixes above were checked in code only and have not been tested in game yet. Please report anything that behaves differently from its description.
- The new animations were reviewed outside the game; this is their first release in play. Known blemishes: the Fangarm's far wing can spike, Harpy wings show a small hook at the root, Gog-line tails dip below the feet when starting and stopping, the Pit Fiend's whip shows thin tears while moving, and the Mammoths and one Armadillo variant changed only slightly.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals. Royal Griffin, Green and Gold Dragon and Vampire Lord are being reworked separately.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.

Nightly builds are untested prereleases. If the problem persists on that build, please reply on the issue tracker.
