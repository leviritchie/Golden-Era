# Golden Era v0.1.334 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** The new animations were checked in renders and automated tests but not played in game, so some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

> **Same Steam build as v0.1.333:** Olden Era **26615913** (depot 3105441, manifest 2034879257573334098). To update an existing Golden Era copy, run this installer and choose **Update an existing Golden Era copy**.

Coming from v0.1.332 or earlier? This release also contains everything in [v0.1.333](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/v0.1.333).

## What changed since v0.1.333

### Units: new animations for the remaining factions

- Rebuilt animations for 154 more units: Bulwark (21), Conflux (19), Cove (21), Factory (24), Inferno (20), Stronghold (20), the neutrals (15), plus the Castle griffins, pikemen and halberdiers, the Rampart pegasi, and the Tower giants and gremlins. Movement, idle, attack, defend, hit and death motions are new, and damage timing follows the new attacks.
- The Roc has a new flight and wing rig.
- Each rebuilt unit keeps the HoMM3-graded texture from v0.1.333: the graded paint was carried over onto the rebuilt meshes, so colours match the previous release.
- Unit effects (breath, staff bolts, hit sparks and similar) were re-attached to the new skeletons.

## Known issues

- Not yet rebuilt: Gargoyle and Obsidian Gargoyle, Glaivier, Energy and Plasma Elemental, Azure Dragon, Efreet Sultan's alternate upgrade, and Wolf Rider keep their previous animations.
- Devils and Arch Devils walk instead of teleporting, so the teleport cloud plays around a visible body.
- A few rebuilt deaths (Automaton line) hold a pose for one or two frames longer than intended.
- Face problems found in the v0.1.333 review are not fixed in this release; improved faces are being prepared for a later one.
- Where a rebuilt mesh has new geometry (mostly some Bulwark yetis, rams and jotunns), that part uses the rebuild's own paint, which can differ slightly in tone from the rest of the unit.
- Gold Pegasus's saddle cloth is red and white where the original sprite is gold and dark.
- Diamond Dragon has small holes in its wings.
