# Golden Era v0.1.329 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

> **Same Steam build as v0.1.328:** Olden Era **26615913** (depot 3105441, manifest 2034879257573334098). To update an existing Golden Era copy, run this installer and choose **Update an existing Golden Era copy**.

## What changed since v0.1.328

### Much faster launch

- The game now reaches the mod about 70 seconds sooner. The mod loader was searching every one of the mod's asset folders for each of the game's 142 code libraries; a small loader patch (`BepInEx/patchers/GoldenEraFastResolve.dll`) replaces that search with a one-time index.
- Plugin startup, menu loading and several in-battle and map hot paths do less repeated work.
- Glowing unit effects use about an eighth of the memory they did before.

### Cove: Treasure Hunting replaces Groundsight and Plunder

- Every region of a random map now hides one **buried treasure**: gold and resources, often an artifact, sometimes a stack of nonliving units. Every treasure is worth at least 8000 gold, and richer regions are worth more.
- Each region has **map pieces** hidden in objects and guarded stacks. A Cove hero finds them nearby. The first piece marks four possible tiles, each further piece rules one out, and four pieces pinpoint the site.
- The free **Dig** spell uses most of a day's movement. Digging on the wrong tile leaves a hole.
- The **Treasure Chart** (scroll button on the adventure map, or **Ctrl+T**) shows every region, your pieces, dug and taken sites, and totals. Click a tile to move the map there.
- Subskills: Home Waters, Archivist, Lay of the Land. Expert picks: Collector of Secrets, Tireless Captain, Ancient Curses (Despair for three turns on enemies in regions you dug).
- Computer Cove heroes dig too. Treasure progress is saved with your game.

### Faction and unit changes

- **Fortress, Predator Instinct:** the bonus against prey rises to +33/66/100%. While its prey lives, a predator deals 17/33/50% less damage to other targets.
- **Conflux:** elementals and fused units no longer start battles with Focus. Hovering the fusion result slot previews the fused unit, and right-clicking it opens its glossary entry.
- **Upgrade pairs follow vanilla bookkeeping:** an upgrade or alternate is never weaker than its base in any stat, alternates cost and score like their upgrade, and experience rewards follow vanilla ratios. The Giant line and Firebird were brought back inside vanilla ranges.
- **Laws:** Phoenix Omen (Conflux) now gives +2 Luck, and Goblin Pressgang (Stronghold) makes recruiting 10% cheaper, instead of repeating the law next to them. Custom faction law trees put each law in the right column with art that matches its effect.
- **Free actions:** Aurora Bound, Slither Away, Rivulet Retreat, Wind-Up and Fine Bolts no longer end the unit's turn.
- **Tinker:** Long Reach no longer counts as a shot, and its unit panel no longer lists the same ability twice.
- Hero specialization descriptions now use vanilla wording and describe enemy-side effects correctly.

### Fixes

- Unit ability number keys (1-5) keep working for the whole battle.
- Fusion units and custom neutrals show their portraits in the turn-order bar and unit panel.
- Battle results no longer show the previous battle's unit portrait in empty army slots.
- Closing a town recruit window no longer leaves a grid of "New Text" cards over the town.
- Long Factory logistics enhancement descriptions wrap inside their window.
- Computer players' War Captives choices no longer pop up a result banner on your screen.

## Known issues

- Treasure Hunting works only on randomly generated maps for now; hand-made and HoMM3 maps get it later. Several of its parts (computer digging, click-to-jump, saving) have had only a short test or none.
- Many faction skills from v0.1.328 have still only had a short in-game test. Please report anything that behaves differently from its description.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.
