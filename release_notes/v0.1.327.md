# Golden Era v0.1.327 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

> **New Steam build.** v0.1.327 is built for the current Olden Era Steam build, **26615913** (depot 3105441, manifest 2034879257573334098). v0.1.326 targeted the older build 25672315. If your Steam install is up to date, just point the installer at it. To update an existing Golden Era copy, run this installer and choose **Update an existing Golden Era copy**.

## What changed since v0.1.326

### Updated for Olden Era build 26615913

The mod was ported to the November Olden Era update. Every game hook was re-checked against the new build. Hooks that broke silently were fixed, including faction runes, law owners, the hero stat popup, Tower's Aegis reward and law icons.

### Tower: Artificing replaces Arcane Battery

- Tower heroes now learn **schematics** by watching creatures in battle. After a battle, a notice lists what was added to the schematic book.
- A world spell opens the Artificing picker, styled like the Laws window, to graft schematics onto the hero's army. It includes a short how-it-works primer.
- **Arcane Essence** is shown in the energy frame beside the hero portrait.
- **Gold Producer** and **Taxpayer** grafts now actually pay out each day, at a flat Tier 1 rate. Before, they scaled with the creature's tier and were far too strong.
- Tower law texts were rewritten to match what the laws really do. The sight building is now called **Lookout Tower**.

### Animations

- A clip pass fixed the non-movement animations (idle, mouseover, attack, defend, hit and death) for about 145 units. Where needed, their meshes were repaired first.

### Abilities

- A code review fixed a set of defects shared by many custom unit abilities. It also adds the missing data behind several abilities: Giants' mind immunity, Fangarm Focus and Cyclops range.
- Counterattack laws now work in battle.
- The Necropolis raise popup now only appears for your own heroes and shows the hero's name.

### Other fixes

- The town hero card and the battle result screen show the right hero portraits again. A custom portrait no longer sticks on a vanilla hero.
- Halflings and Boars no longer wander the map as neutrals. The factions that recruit them still do.
- Bug reports send as soon as you press Submit, and no longer get stuck on "Sending".

## Known issues

- The Gold Producer fix and the ability fixes were checked in code only and have not been tested in game yet. Please report anything that behaves differently from its description.
- The campaigns have not been re-checked on the new game build.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.
