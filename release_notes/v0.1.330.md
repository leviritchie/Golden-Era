## What changed since v0.1.329

### Stronghold: Warcry replaces the three Warcries

The hero no longer picks Rallying, Fortune or Tempo Cry. Instead, the whole army builds a **Warcry** by fighting without pause.

- Each attack or ability used by a friendly stack, one after another, adds 1 to the **Warcry counter**, shown on screen during battle.
- Every stack that attacks in melee **joins the Warcry**. Ranged attacks keep the Warcry going but don't raise the counter or join it.
- A Stronghold creature beginning its turn gains **Attack** for that turn equal to the counter multiplied by the number of stacks in the Warcry: x1 at Basic, x1.5 at Advanced, x2 at Expert.
- Moving without attacking, waiting or defending **breaks the Warcry**.
- Advanced subskills: **Native Drums** (+10% damage on native terrain), **Shaman's Fury** (the bonus is added to the hero's Spell Power), **Battle Tempo** (+2 Initiative for the round while the Warcry is active).
- Expert subskills: **Answering Roar** (retaliating stacks join the Warcry), **Blood Rush** (destroying an enemy stack adds 5 to the bonus), **Ancestral Voices** (fallen allied stacks keep counting for the rest of the battle).

Everything in [v0.1.329](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/v0.1.329) (faster launch, Cove Treasure Hunting and more) is included.

## Known issues

- Warcry has had a short in-game test. Shaman's Fury and Battle Tempo have not been checked in play yet.
- Treasure Hunting works only on randomly generated maps for now; hand-made and HoMM3 maps get it later. Several of its parts (computer digging, click-to-jump, saving) have had only a short test or none.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.
