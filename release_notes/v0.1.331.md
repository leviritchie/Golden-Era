# Golden Era v0.1.331 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** It still has issues, and some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

> **Same Steam build as v0.1.330:** Olden Era **26615913** (depot 3105441, manifest 2034879257573334098). To update an existing Golden Era copy, run this installer and choose **Update an existing Golden Era copy**.

## What changed since v0.1.330

### Units: rebalance and new abilities for all 12 factions

- **Rebalance:** every faction's units are re-statted to the vanilla tier bands with a shared power model. Units with strong abilities pay for them with weaker base stats. Upgrades and alternate upgrades trade off differently in each faction.
- **Ability variety:** no two units in the same faction share the same ability card anymore. Generic resistances became unit-flavoured conditional abilities, and repeated "+damage, no retaliation" and long-thrust copies were replaced with new abilities.
- **New battle rules** behind those abilities: turn state, retaliation state, first hit, adjacency, extra action after a kill and more. Undead, demon, shooter and flyer class tags let abilities target unit classes.
- **Wished rules:** Hatred, rotating Wish, Braced Pikes, army Morale, Frostbite into Frozen, Fire Shield, elemental weaknesses, Harpies that always return, Revenge and Grave Tide.
- **Castle pikemen:** Set Pikes holds only until the stack acts (Wait and Defend keep it). The Halberdier loses Sweeping Halberd, and the Glaivier's Guillotine returns in place of Long Thrust.
- **Rampart Avenger rework:** allied stacks make avenging strikes when the army is attacked; ranged stacks strike with their melee attack.
- **Focus generation** for custom units follows vanilla: 2 per melee action, 1 per ranged action.
- **Card icons** follow the vanilla design language: passive abilities get gold glyphs, active abilities and alternative attacks get painted icons. Icons missing from vanilla use the closest vanilla art.
- **Artificing** follows the new ability cards.
- Elemental Fusion creatures get fluid bodies and new looks.

### Languages

- All custom text (unit cards and buffs, Artificing, laws, hero skills including Warcry and Avenger, map objects, campaign and hero bios) is now translated into all 15 languages instead of showing English.

### Fixes

- Switching from one town to another rebuilds the town scene; the previous town's scenery no longer stays under the new town.
- The town door/back button closes an open build, hire or upgrade screen before leaving town.
- The Quick Play faction grid no longer fails to appear at random.
- Melee hits and shots land in time with the attack animations.
- The post-battle summary lists units in the battle's side order.

Everything in [v0.1.330](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/v0.1.330) (Stronghold Warcry and more) is included.

## Known issues

- The rebalance and the new abilities have not been tested in play yet. Some abilities may not trigger, or may behave differently from their card text. Please report what you find.
- The new translations are machine-assisted. Some names may differ from the official HoMM translations, and a few wordings may be awkward.
- Not included yet, because they need new or rebuilt meshes: Black, Red, Rust and Azure Dragons, Shadow Dragon, Ghost Dragon, Wyvern Monarch, Paokai, Firebird and Sacred Phoenix flight, plus Beholder, Enchanter, Steel Golem, Champion, Naga Queen variant, and the Magic and Ice Elementals.
- Treasure Hunting works only on randomly generated maps for now.
- The Conflux town scene has not been rebuilt yet and still uses the older version.
- Town scenes can still show small streaks at some camera angles.
- Known issues from earlier releases still apply unless noted here.
