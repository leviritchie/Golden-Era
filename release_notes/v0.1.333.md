# Golden Era v0.1.333 (Preview)

> **This is a preview build, not a finished release.** Many of these changes were checked in files and renders but not fully played in game, so some problems will only show up in play.

> **Want the newest fixes?** The [nightly build](https://github.com/leviritchie/Golden-Era/releases/tag/nightly) is rebuilt automatically as fixes land. It has more fixes than this release but is less validated: nightlies are not hand-tested. If a bug blocks you here, try the nightly.

## What changed since v0.1.332

### Units: new textures

- New 2K textures for the HoMM3 units across all 12 factions and the neutrals, with colours and brightness matched to the original HoMM3 sprites. HotA alternate upgrades use their own recolours. Wolf Rider keeps its previous look.

### Units: new animations

- Rebuilt animations for 108 units: Castle (15), Rampart (18), Tower (12), Dungeon (21), Necropolis (21) and Fortress (21). Movement, idle, attack, defend, hit and death motions are new, and damage timing follows the new attacks.
- Necropolis units that used to shatter into rubble on death now collapse instead.
- Castle pikemen, halberdiers and glaiviers get the new textures but keep their previous animations. The other factions' animations are still being worked on.

### Faction laws

- A second pass over the laws of all 12 factions, with effects that are the faction's own instead of repeating hero skills, vanilla-style wording and translations.
- Bulwark: the five Rune subskills Rune Ward, Clan March, Frost Tempo, Elder Mark and Stone Circle now have real effects.

### Fixes

- The AI turn could freeze (reported on day 2) because some hero specialties and artifacts granted resources Olden Era does not have. They now grant Olden Era resources.
- Harpies with Hit and Run return to where they started after attacking.
- Ergon restores 5 Movement after battles.
- Lizard Assassin's movement speed is corrected.

## Known issues

- Gold Pegasus's saddle cloth is red and white where the original sprite is gold and dark.
- The base Genie has a slightly flat patch on one sleeve; the Naga line has small dark texture patches.
- Diamond Dragon has small holes in its wings.
